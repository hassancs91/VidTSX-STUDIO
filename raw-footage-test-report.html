The editorial pass earned its name

This was the standout. On clip 1 the agent found the spoken slate “Another take.”, cut the superseded delivery before it, killed four false starts and a junk tail, reported “no um/uh anywhere — nothing to do on filler” instead of inventing work, and flagged a genuine judgment call:

“Look at this line. is said twice at 1:43 and 1:45. I cut the first per doubled-phrase rules, but if you're pointing at two different lines on screen that's deliberate — veto it.”

It also disclosed which cut boundaries were estimated (segment-level timing) and asked for those joins to be auditioned. On clip 3 it kept a doubled “we should adapt / you should adapt” pair flagged as a possible intentional beat. This is editor-grade reasoning, not pattern deletion.

Proof frames

Findings

CrashApp exited (code 255) during shot generation — recovery flawless

RAM sat at 95–99% all session (4K HEVC preview + libx264 proxy encodes + two claude.exe agent runs); the process died right as the two freshly generated shot modules were being served. Looks like an OOM kill. The good news is the recovery story: every applied cut, both ready shots, and the open shot-plan proposal survived the restart — reopening the project put the “Place 2 shots” review right back on screen. Worth a memory-pressure investigation before v1; the persistence design already earns its keep.

UX“Transcribing… 0%” while actually queued behind proxy builds

Transcript jobs share the 2-slot job engine with proxy builds, so on a no-NVENC machine all three transcriptions showed a motionless 0% for minutes. Reads as stuck/failed. A “Queued…” label (or letting transcripts jump the proxy queue — they gate the whole editing flow) would fix the impression.

UXUnanchored shots land at 0:00 on the overlay lane

The end card was proposed with “no timelineStart — drag to the tail,” and placement dropped it at 0:00, covering the opening five seconds with an opaque card until moved. Auto-appending unanchored cutaways after the last master clip would match intent better.

UXProject card footer “Open” opens the cache folder, not the project

The card's footer row reads “Cache 18 KB · Open · Clear”. Automation (and plausibly a hurried user) hits “Open” expecting to open the project and gets an Explorer window. “Show in Explorer” or an icon would disambiguate.

MinorSnap missed by 0.12s on a zoomed-out drag

Dragging the end card to the timeline tail at ~5 px/s zoom landed at 142.172s vs the 142.05s edge — inside the snap's pixel threshold arithmetic but visible as a 4-frame gap. Frame-quantizing commits (or widening snap at low zoom) would close it.

NoteAnchor remapping through heavy cuts works

The intro title was anchored to source words in clip 2 (0271 @ 22.1–26.4s). After 121 cuts across the timeline it landed at exactly the right spot over the keeper take — the source-anchored proposal design (D8 Rev 3) doing what it promised.

KeysAssemblyAI rotated key is live in-app; LLM keys likely still stale

The rotated AssemblyAI key was entered through the real Providers UI during this test and proved out against the live API. The OpenRouter / MiniMax / Kimi rows still show “Key saved” from before the 2026-08-18 rotation — those are probably revoked values and will fail until re-entered. (Claude Subscription needs no key and powered all agent work here.)
