# Style: Flat bold

Thick, friendly vector shapes, saturated and confident. Use the colours by their ROLE everywhere: the accent marks
the payoff, warn marks overload or failure, ok marks the healthy state.

- **Ground:** warm cream #FFF4E3, with one large soft circle of #FFE7C2 behind the main subject.
- **Ink:** #23233B for text and small details. Shapes have NO outlines: flat fills with generous rounded corners
  (24 px on large shapes, about a fifth of the short side on small ones).
- **Palette:** coral #FF5A5F (accent), teal #00A6A6 (ok), sunflower #FFB400, navy #3D5A80, lavender #9B8CD9.
  **Warn:** #E63946.
- **Type:** Poppins from `@remotion/google-fonts/Poppins`: 800 for titles and numbers, 600 for labels, with
  'Segoe UI', Arial, sans-serif after it.
- **Actors:** round blobs with two dot eyes and a small mouth, simple stick arms.
- **Motion:** bouncy and physical: `spring()` with damping 12-18 for arrivals, squash and stretch on landings
  (scaleY 0.85 → 1.08 → 1), pops on counters.
