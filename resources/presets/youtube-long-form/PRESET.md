# YouTube long-form

A long-form video earns attention with a cold open and keeps it with
structure. Cut for clarity, not for speed.

## Cold open

- The first 5–8 seconds are the payoff in miniature: the result, the
  surprising number, or the question the video answers. Pull it forward from
  wherever the speaker says it best, then let the introduction follow.
- One title shot over the cold open, word-synced, showing the video's subject
  in five words or fewer. It is the only shot allowed in the first ten
  seconds.

## Pacing

- Auto Cut runs normal: remove dead air, keep the breathing room between
  sentences — a long-form viewer needs the pauses to think.
- The editorial pass cuts every retake and false start, cuts fillers only
  where the splice is clean, and SUGGESTS fluff rather than removing it. A
  tangent that carries personality stays.
- Aim for one visual change every 30–45 seconds. Longer stretches are fine
  during a story; never during a list.
- Crossfades are allowed between sections (0.5 s); hard cuts inside a
  section.

## Structure

- Find the 3–6 sections in the transcript and name them. Each section opens
  with a lower-third overlay carrying its name (3–4 seconds, anchored to the
  section's first sentence) — the viewer should always know where they are.
- Lists become numbered overlays that appear as each item is spoken.

## Shots and b-roll

- Cutaways illustrate, they do not decorate: a diagram for a mechanism, a
  comparison table for a comparison, a quote card for a quote. If nothing
  needs illustrating, propose nothing.
- B-roll goes where the speaker describes something visual. Search the
  library first; generate only when the library has nothing that fits, and
  say the spend before generating.
- Overlays stay in the lower third or a corner; the speaker's face is the
  anchor of a long-form video.

## Captions

- On by default, a clean single line in the lower third, four words per
  group, sentence case. No uppercase, no bounce.

## Sound

- A quiet music bed under the cold open and the outro only; speech sections
  run dry unless the user asks otherwise.
- Sound effects are rare — one on the title reveal, one on each section card
  at most. If sound tools are unavailable, say so in one line and move on.

## Outro

- Up to ten seconds: the speaker's closing line, one overlay with the next
  video or the call to action, then a crossfade to black.
