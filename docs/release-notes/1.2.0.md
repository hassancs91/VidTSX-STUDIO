## What's new since 1.1.0

**The TSX Builder: animations made the way a designer works.** Every place
that makes a TSX composition (the TSX Creator, Studio shots and the Studio
assistant, agents, and flows) now uses one builder. It designs the picture,
writes the code as files, renders frames, looks at them and redesigns, round
after round, before it hands you the result. You watch it work: the round,
what it is doing, and each frame it looked at. Pick how long it may work:
**Quick** (about 1-3 minutes, one look), **Standard** (about 3-10 minutes, up
to three looks; the default) or **Craft** (up to an hour, up to six looks,
for a finished piece). One build runs at a time; the next one waits its turn,
and Cancel stops a build at any point.

**What it can draw.** 3D (three.js and React Three Fiber) and physics are in,
alongside the 2D libraries, all bundled with the app: no network needed after
the first run. 3D frames render on your GPU. Physics is replayed frame by
frame, so the preview and the export always match.

**Craft packs: kinds and looks.** The Creator gains **Kind** and **Style**.
Kind = *Infographic loop* makes an animated infographic post (one whole
picture on every frame, a seamless loop, numbers drawn as things you can
count) and sets 4:5, 8 seconds, loop and Craft for you. Style picks a look:
Blueprint, Flat bold, Neon dark, Editorial, or your own words. Agents and the
flow node use the same packs.

**The builder checks its own work.** Before it may finish, it must have
looked at the final build and at the smallest text on a phone-sized frame; a
loop must join seamlessly; physics must replay identically; and numbers that
change on screen must agree with what the picture shows, including a race
whose two sides must run on one clock. When a check still fails after three
tries, the build finishes anyway and says which check is not verified. A free
overlap check lists text that collides with other text or with a shape.

**See the cost before you start.** Next to the budget, the Creator and Studio
show what a build will cost: "≈ $low–high" with an Anthropic API key or an
OpenRouter model, "on your plan" with a Claude subscription, "cost unknown"
where there is no price list. On Claude Sonnet 5.5 a build costs about
$0.30-0.95 whatever the budget; Opus 5.5 at Craft about $1.55-3.40.

**Creator.** The composer builds on the first message; later messages, the
Edit box and "Fix with AI" are edits of the open version, saved as a new
version beside it. New 4:5 aspect ratio. Auto length lets the builder choose.

**Studio.** Generating, regenerating, editing and Refine on a TSX shot all go
through the builder, with a budget picker (and its cost) in the media pool
form and the Inspector. 3D shots work in Studio. Convert for Studio is a
Quick builder edit, and a dropped-in multi-file shot is bundled in place.
Shots play at their own frame rate and size, scaled to the frame.

**Agents.** `generate_composition` and `edit_composition` build through the
builder and return the frames it last looked at, so an agent can judge the
result without a render. They take craft packs and can rebuild an earlier
composition at another budget. An agent package can ship its own builder
know-how (`builder: true` skills). A card you skip answers with your next
message. The TSX Composer agent is retired: the Creator now does its job
directly. Package authors: `docs/AGENT_PACKAGE_SPEC.md`.

**Flows.** The Generate Composition node gains Budget, Loop, Kind and Style,
and "Generated images" (off unless you turn it on). A checkpoint you reject
with a note edits the rejected version instead of starting over.

**Also:** exports use the GPU backend automatically where it helps; Claude
defaults to Sonnet 5.5; a tool-permission gap in the agent runtime is closed.

## Known issues (draft)

- Craft builds can take up to an hour, and the whole app builds one piece at
  a time.
- The cost hint covers the builder's tokens only, not generated images. The
  OpenRouter estimate comes from OpenRouter's list prices and has not yet been
  checked against an OpenRouter bill.
- The numbers check reads the text on screen: it can tell that "2 s" and
  "2 ms" race on two clocks, but not a race between unlike units (rows read
  vs. steps).
- The overlap check does not read z-index, or text over images, canvas or 3D.
- Craft packs are built in only; a `.vidtsxpack` cannot add one yet.
- Earlier sessions of the retired TSX Composer agent no longer appear.
