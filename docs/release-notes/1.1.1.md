# VidTSX Studio 1.1.1

A small fix release.

## Fixes

- **Studio:** a TSX shot placed from the media pool now goes by the shot's
  name instead of "tsx", on its timeline clip, in the Inspector and in the
  Transitions tab. A clip with nothing else to show reads "Shot", "Captions"
  and so on.
- **Clearer refusals:** when a template, an agent package or a transition
  pack is refused for importing a package the app does not allow, the message
  now says "a composition may only import from 'react', 'remotion', and
  '@vidtsx/kit'" instead of talking about shots.

## Updating from 1.1.0

Nothing to do. About 30 seconds after launch the app finds this update and
downloads it in the background. It installs the next time you quit, or right
away from the "Restart" chip or Settings, Updates, "Restart & install".

## Known issues

- The installer is not code-signed yet. Windows SmartScreen shows a warning
  on a fresh install; choose "More info", then "Run anyway". Updates install
  without it.
- If you reload the app while a video generation is running, its progress
  card disappears. The job keeps running and the clip still lands in the
  library.
- Cost figures in the usage dashboard are estimates at list rate for most
  models.
