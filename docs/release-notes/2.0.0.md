## What's new

**Interior staging reel, a new built-in flow.** Drop in one photo of a room,
pick an image model and a video model, and get a vertical 9:16 reel of the
room being furnished: the empty room fills with furniture, then a slow glide
through it at golden hour. Checkpoints, the default, shows you each still
before the paid video starts; Unattended runs straight through. It needs one
fal key, and a reel costs about $2.25 on the defaults (Nano Banana Pro and
Kling 3 Pro). Six built-in flows now ship.

**Kling 3 and Kling 4 on fal.** Kling 3 Pro, Kling 3 Standard and Kling 4
join the video models. They take a start and an end frame, so a clip can run
from one picture to another.

**Join clips, a new flow node.** Joins videos into one, with a cut or a dip
to black, at the size and frame rate you set.

**Signing.** The built-in flows are signed with a new VidTSX key. Nothing to
do: they read "Verified by VidTSX" as before.
